Welcome to the course website! This site hosts materials for UC San Diego CSE 150A – Introduction to Artificial Intelligence: Probabilistic Reasoning and Decision-Making and CSE 250A – Principles of Artificial Intelligence: Probabilistic Reasoning and Learning (Fall 2026). Here you’ll find all essential information, materials, and resources for the quarter.

## About the Course

This course introduces probabilistic and statistical models at the heart of modern artificial intelligence, including reasoning under uncertainty, Bayesian networks, and Markov Decision Processes.

First day of instruction: `Thursday, Sep 24 at 12:30PM PT` in [JEANN](https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1005#!s/JEANN_Main?ct/18312)
